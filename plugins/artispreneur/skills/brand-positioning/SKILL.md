# Brand Positioning

The one true thing about you that no other artist in your genre can say.

Artispreneur skill `brand-positioning` · Studio · Content Director · about 20 minutes with the artist.

## What the artist gets

- One-paragraph positioning statement
- 3 tagline options
- Voice guide: 3 adjectives + 1 sentence on tone
- Story structure: origin → tension → proof → mission

## Ask first

- Your genre and influences
- Your story — where you're from and what shaped you
- Who you make music for

## Steps

1. Find your authentic angle
2. Write the positioning statement
3. Draft taglines
4. Define your voice

## Guardrails

- Never invent facts, numbers, contacts, or credits — ask, or leave it out.
- No generic claims a thousand artists could make

## Playbook

This is the Artispreneur agent playbook for this skill, unchanged from the app.

Goal: positioning every other skill (EPK, bio, content, PR) can inherit.
1. Interview briefly: genre, influences, origin, a defining moment, who the music is for, what fans say about it.
2. Use a specific true angle only when supplied. Sparse facts can support provisional creative positioning, not invented uniqueness, origin, credentials or fan behavior. Missing research means research not supplied, not proof no research exists.
3. Follow requested count/format for alternative positioning statements. Audience/use-case ideas are unvalidated hypotheses, never demographic facts or claims of proven resonance.
4. Tagline and messaging examples must use only supplied titles, recording details, dates, releases and links. Never invent a track name, one-take/no-edit story, recording hour, origin, link-in-bio or new release inside an example. Use [TRACK TITLE TO CONFIRM] placeholders or timeless factual copy.
5. Voice: proposed adjectives and examples of style, not fabricated biography. Story structure origin/tension/proof/mission can remain [NOT SUPPLIED]. Do not add false accuracy assurances to the footer.
6. Validation experiment must respect the requested audience/effects boundary. For no external posting/surveys, propose private copy comparison or review using already-owned material, not DSP uploads, recruitment or account changes. Do not invent success benchmarks or require500organicstreams; define measurements and how the artist could compare results without promising validity.
7. Call create_document (type "doc", skill "brand-positioning") titled "Brand positioning — <name>".

## Running it inside Claude
