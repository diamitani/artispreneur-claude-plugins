# Music Licensing Brief

Pitch-ready sync briefs and licensing basics, in plain English.

Artispreneur skill `license-music` · Contracts · Business Manager · about 15 minutes with the artist.

## What the artist gets

- Sync one-sheet per track (mood, tempo, themes, clearances)
- Licensing terms explained (master vs. sync, fees, territory)
- Pitch email template for music supervisors

## Ask first

- Tracks you want to license and who owns the master/publishing
- Mood, themes, and comparable artists
- Any existing licensing deals

## Steps

1. Confirm you control both sides (or who does)
2. Write track one-sheets
3. Explain terms you'll be offered
4. Write the pitch

## Guardrails

- Flags any track you can't clear one-stop
- General information, not legal advice — review anything binding with a lawyer.

## Playbook

This is the Artispreneur agent playbook for this skill, unchanged from the app.

Goal: a proposed brief matching the actual requested use, not automatically sync.
1. Use supplied tracks, master/composition ownership and splits. Artist-reported ownership is not independent clearance; mark one-stop status claimed and pending checks for publishers, prior grants, samples and other rights.
2. Track title, duration, BPM, mood and instrumental availability only if supplied or verified. Missing metadata stays unknown, never infer from the title or fictional identity.
3. An audio-only podcast is not automatically audiovisual sync. Preserve use, term, territory and exclusivity as proposals. Missing edit/loop/sublicense/renewal terms are open questions, not legal defaults. Do not invent fee ranges, standard warranties, cue-sheet requirements or PRO royalty eligibility. Verify current source and jurisdiction for any rule or omit it.
4. Counterparty identity, distribution platforms, fee and contact remain unknown unless supplied. Do not search unrelated contacts for a clearly separate fictional exercise; a directory listing alone is not a verified recipient.
5. Draft inquiry only, with unknown names/contact placeholders and no unverified clearance promises or asset offers. Never send, negotiate, accept, sign or register. Call create_document (type "pitch", skill "license-music"). General information, not legal advice — review anything binding with a lawyer.

## Running it inside Claude

Return a draft in the conversation using supplied facts only. Mark missing facts [CONFIRM]. Do not call app-only tools, access accounts, save, send, publish, file, sign or pay. Catalog tools retrieve instructions only; planning tools (release readiness, release plan, EPK outline) compute checklists from what the artist supplies, not approvals. No tool executes a skill or builds an artifact. Legal and tax content is general information for professional review.

- Saving to the Artispreneur Library (`create_document`) is app-only: deliver the finished document in the conversation instead.
- The live Artispreneur industry directory (`search_directory`) is app-only: never invent contacts; work from contacts the artist supplies, or point them to the Artispreneur app.
